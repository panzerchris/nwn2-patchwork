Date: 2026-10-29. **Keep this release in draft.** Microsoft Defender quarantined
the CI-built launcher during its first runtime check. The successor 0.2.0 drop-in
DLL is published separately; do not publish this candidate as ready to use.

## Microsoft case update, checked 2026-10-29

The original ZIP and explanation were submitted and the case now says Completed.
Analysts report that their scanners cannot reproduce a positive detection and
they have no telemetry indicators for the submitted files. They closed the case
without further action. Current cloud/client detection is No malware detected
(client definitions 1.463.24.0), while the file-level final-determination cell
still says Pending. Do not collapse those distinct portal fields into a claimed
explicit false-positive correction or guaranteed behavior-rule change.

Microsoft requests current security intelligence and a diagnostic bundle from
the affected machine if the behavior detection recurs and further investigation
is wanted. No such bundle was collected or uploaded in this status check. Private
case receipt/account details remain under local/, outside Git. The original
runtime/quarantine evidence below is unchanged and this launcher remains held.
